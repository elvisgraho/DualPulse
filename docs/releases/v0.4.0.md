## What's new

- **Microphone in games:** in DualSense mode on the cable, games hear your controller's microphone through the
  virtual DualSense, with a noise gate that ignores mouth clicks, a low cut and a boost.
- **Microphone tab:** live sound of your controller's mic, which Windows device to pick in games, its volume, and the
  Mute button settings (moved from Lighting).
- **Feel PC sound on the cable:** now through the controller's haptics instead of Bluetooth only, with less delay.
- **Controller details:** firmware and connected time under the controller picture; Details shows everything, with
  Copy. The picture shows a cable when plugged in.
- **DualSense mode:** choose whether a game sets the lights and adaptive triggers. The Emulation page says in short
  what each mode gives.
- **New installs start in Native**, which needs no driver.

## Fixes

- The Mute button mutes the microphone again.
- Plugging in the cable no longer moves your PC's sound to the controller.
- Firmware is read again when Bluetooth connects right after the controller is switched on.
