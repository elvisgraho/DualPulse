## What's new

- **Steam Input notice:** when Steam Input is on for the game you're playing, Home names the game and opens it in
  Steam, so you can turn Steam Input off there (it can take your adaptive triggers and lights).

## Fixes

- The game microphone keeps a small cushion, so short gaps and crackles from timing are gone.
- The controller's microphone comes back by itself after Windows resets its sound device.
- The Microphone tab always closes the microphone when you leave it.
- The microphone volume slider no longer jumps back after you move it.
- The noise gate's line on the waveform shows where your voice actually opens it.
- If you chose the controller as your sound output, plugging it in again keeps it.
- A few rare crashes and leaks around the microphone and the sound output fixed.
