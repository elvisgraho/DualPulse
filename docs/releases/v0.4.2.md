## What's new

- **Controller shortcuts:** hold PS and press the D-pad. Right/Left switch profile, Up/Down switch the adaptive trigger
  preset. The player LEDs show which one. Games don't see PS or the D-pad while you use a shortcut. Switch it off in
  Settings.
- **Profiles:** import and export a profile as a file, and rename it, from the ⋯ menu on the Controller page.
- New profile, Add game and Rename open in the middle of the page.

## Fixes

- The Controller page follows a profile you switch to by hand.
