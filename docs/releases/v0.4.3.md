## What's new

- **Sound device for games:** a switch on the Emulation page (on by default). Off, the virtual DualSense is the
  controller alone, without its sound device.
- **Reconnect controller** on the Emulation page, for when a game sees two controllers. Over Bluetooth it turns the
  controller off (press PS to turn it back on); on the cable it asks you to plug it in again.

## Fixes

- The Mute button now really silences the microphone. Before, Windows showed it muted but games and apps still heard
  you. Windows also shows the virtual DualSense's microphone muted.
