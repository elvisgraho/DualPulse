# Privacy policy

DualPulse does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or ads.

**What stays on your PC.** Settings and logs are stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DualPulse` and never leave your PC. To work,
DualPulse reads your controller's input, the name of the program in front (for game profiles) and, when you turn on
the sound features, your PC's sound (to play it on the controller or turn it into haptics). This is processed on your
PC and not stored.

**Network.** The only internet connection DualPulse makes is the update check, and only if you turn on "Tell me when
there's a new version" in Settings. It asks GitHub for the latest release once a day; GitHub sees your IP address and
DualPulse's version, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The mod server and the virtual DualSense accept connections only from your own PC.

**Website.** The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses (see GitHub's privacy
statement). It uses no cookies or trackers of its own.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/elvisgraho/DualPulse/issues.
